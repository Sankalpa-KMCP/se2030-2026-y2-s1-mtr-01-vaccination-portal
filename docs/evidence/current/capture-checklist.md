# Current capture checklist

MISSING — capture required during final evidence assembly. Use synthetic data and label every capture with baseline commit, date, role and expected/observed outcome. Never retain passwords, OTPs, bearer headers, cookie values or personal medical data.

| Function | Minimum useful captures |
|---|---|
| MF01 | Citizen form/version; preliminary result; officer correction/review and final eligibility; duplicate/ownership rejection |
| MF02 | Campaign publication; center/resources/staff planning; session capacity/readiness; rejected conflict and change impact |
| MF03 | Availability and booking; mine/reschedule/cancel; FIFO waitlist promotion; staff check-in; full-slot or unauthorized rejection |
| MF04 | Batch and center stock; adjustment audit; invalid/immutable identity rejection; stock before/after finalization |
| MF05 | Draft; finalization result/record; certificate and masked verification; rollback/duplicate rejection; correction/reissue history |

Shared supporting captures: cold bootstrap, authenticated reload, logout, different-user cross-tab rejection with zero replay and same-user renewal. Use identity labels only. Sanitized network status/counts may demonstrate behavior without revealing tokens. CI run links accompany screenshots; screenshots alone do not prove concurrency. Capture actual board/meeting/review evidence separately; do not stage fictional Scrum events.
