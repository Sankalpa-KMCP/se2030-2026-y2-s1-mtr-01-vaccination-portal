## 1. Function scope

Screening and Eligibility. Classification: VERIFIED_FINAL_BY_USER_CONSTRAINT. Exactly five assessed functions; shared capabilities remain supporting evidence.

## 2. Actors/roles

Citizen; Healthcare Officer.

## 3. Primary workflow

Select campaign screening, submit the versioned form, inspect preliminary result; Healthcare Officer reviews, requests corrections or records a campaign-specific eligibility decision.

## 4. Key business rules

Preliminary evaluation is not a clinical decision. Submitted answers retain their questionnaire version. Ownership and active-submission uniqueness are enforced.
