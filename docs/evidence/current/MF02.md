Campaign, Center and Session. Classification: VERIFIED_FINAL_BY_USER_CONSTRAINT. Exactly five assessed functions; shared capabilities remain supporting evidence.

## 2. Actors/roles

Campaign Officer; Center Manager; additional administrative/management access is endpoint-specific.

## 3. Primary workflow

Configure campaign/reference/dose programme and questionnaire linkage; assign centers; plan operating hours, resources, staff and sessions; calculate capacity, schedule, inspect readiness and manage changes/recurrence.

## 4. Key business rules

Scheduling rechecks readiness. Resource/staff conflicts and capacity rules apply. Schedule changes preserve affected-booking responsibilities through the Appointment provider boundary.
