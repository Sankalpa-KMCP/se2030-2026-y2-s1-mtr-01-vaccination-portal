## 1. Function scope

## 2. Actors/roles

Inventory Officer and Admin; vaccination deduction is a provider operation used by MF-05.

## 3. Primary workflow

Manage manufacturers/batches, inspect center stock, perform supported stock receipts/adjustments and inspect audit transactions; supply batch availability and finalization deduction.

Stock transfer is not currently implemented and is outside the demonstrated MF04 workflow.

## 4. Key business rules

Draft editing does not change stock. Finalization deduction joins the caller transaction, revalidates availability under locks and records a unique vaccination deduction. Do not assume every planned inventory capability is present.
